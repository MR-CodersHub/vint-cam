1. Trade-In Appraisal Policies

By submitting equipment for trade-in appraisal, you acknowledge that the final payout quote is subject to physical inspection. Gear must match the condition profile declared in the online submission form.

2. Warranty Execution Bounds

Our standard 6-month warranty covers all mechanical functions, shutter operations, and lens aperture mechanisms for purchased cameras. It does not apply to accidental drop damage, water contact, or user misuse.

3. Trade-In Shipments and Appraisals

Trade-in items sent to our boutique must be shipped within 14 business days of receiving the appraisal estimate. Items received after this window may be subject to re-appraisal based on current market inventory levels.
