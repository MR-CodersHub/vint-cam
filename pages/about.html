Our Core Philosophy

Keeping the mechanical
soul of photography alive.

In an era of disposable digital files, we build a physical sanctuary for slow, intentional imagery. We believe that turning a precision gear, setting a split-image rangefinder patch, and chemical synthesis offer an unmatched artistic experience.

Our Journey Through Light

2012 — The Workbench Origins

Started as a single camera repair bench in Brooklyn, rebuilding custom leaf-shutters for local street photographers using discarded spare component lots.

2018 — Opening The Vault Fridge

Expanded into temperature-controlled logistics, rescuing rare deep-frozen master film batches from international government archives before decommissioning updates.

2026 — The Modern Calibration Hub

Now operating a premier mechanical restoration shop, certifying vintage optical equipment with modern multi-year guarantees.
